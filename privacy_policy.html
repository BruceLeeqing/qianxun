QianXun Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to QianXun ("the App"). We highly value your privacy protection. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

2. Information Collection

The information we collect includes:

  • Account Information: Username, profile picture, gender, and other basic information.
  • Voice Data: Audio data generated during voice chat.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and other technical data.
  • Usage Data: App usage frequency, feature usage patterns, and related analytics.

3. Information Usage

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Providing voice chat and social features.
  • Improving user experience and product functionality.
  • Ensuring the safe and stable operation of the App.
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Information may only be shared under the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent.
  • When necessary to provide services you have requested.
  • To comply with laws, regulations, or government requests.
  • To protect our legitimate rights and interests.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and correct your personal information.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Restrict the use of your personal information.
  • Withdraw previously granted consents.

7. Child Safety and CSAE Prevention Standards

The App strictly prohibits any content or behavior related to Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Our commitments include:

  • Users are strictly prohibited from posting, distributing, or sharing any text, images, videos, voice messages, or other content involving child sexual abuse and exploitation.
  • Upon discovery of any such violating content, we will immediately remove it and ban the associated account, and report to relevant authorities (such as the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children, NCMEC, or local law enforcement) in accordance with applicable laws.
  • Users can report suspected CSAE content via the in-app reporting feature or by sending an email to privacy@qianxun.com. All reports will be promptly reviewed and addressed.
  • The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on this page. Please check back periodically for any changes.
